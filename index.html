About LouisGlass consultant and expert witness

Louis brings more than 30 years of international management and technical experience in the glass industry.

His work spans glass melting, ERP system set-up, high-performance sputtered coatings, glazing for large buildings and glass fabrication — across North America and Europe.

In this last decade of his career, he wants to help make the planet more sustainable through glass.

    Plant start-up

    Fabrication · Heat treatment · Equipment debugging

  5. 2006 – 2008

    AGC Flat Glass Company

    World CSR

    Promoted safety, quality and environmental principles across 245 plants and 27,000 workers.

    Work safety · Global leadership · Japanese culture

  6. 2004 – 2006

    AGC Flat Glass Europe

    Sales Manager Europe

    Coordinated sales and production for major European architectural projects, working with architects and façade makers and writing supply contracts.

    Large building project management

  7. 2000 – 2004

    Glaverbel Coating SA

    Sales Manager Europe

    Led sales of high-performance solar-control coated glass and managed European agents. Helped develop extra-clear glass, low solar factor and temperable coatings, and silicon-argon insulated units.

    Coating · Building envelope · Architecture · Sales networks

  8. 1998 – 2000

    Glavinfo Inc.

    IT systems conversion

    Converted the IT systems of five European automotive replacement glass distribution centres after the acquisition of PPG Glass assets — and avoided the Year 2000 bug.

    IT · Project & subcontractor management · European culture

  9. 1995 – 1998

    AFG Industries Ltd.

    Canadian Customer Service Manager

    Managed two customer teams handling about $80 million in sales and 150,000 tonnes of glass shipped.

    Team building · Logistics

  10. 1992 – 1995

    AFG Industries Ltd.
